Hi, I’m Sofiia 👋

UX Designer · Calgary, Canada

I’m a UX Designer who genuinely enjoys making complicated things feel simple. Currently, I design digital experiences for Critical Mass, bringing curiosity, research, and a human perspective to every problem I work on. My path from linguistics to beauty and eventually UX has always been about understanding people and creating meaningful experiences. When I’m not designing, you’ll find me outdoors: skiing and hiking the Rockies.
